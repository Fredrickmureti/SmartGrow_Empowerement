Enterprise ERP & Accounting Platform
=====================================

Designed and built a full-stack, production-grade ERP and double-entry accounting system serving as a unified business management platform for SMEs and growing enterprises.

Core Platform
─────────────
• Multi-tenant architecture with strict per-business data isolation, row-level security, and role-based access control across organizations, businesses, and branches.
• Real-time collaboration with live presence indicators, WebSocket-powered data sync, and optimistic UI updates.
• Subscription and permission gating with tiered feature access.

Financial Accounting (Odoo/QuickBooks-grade)
────────────────────────────────────────────
• Full double-entry General Ledger with hierarchical chart of accounts, journal entries, and automatic balancing.
• Financial reporting suite: Balance Sheet, Income Statement (P&L), Cash Flow (indirect method), Trial Balance, General Ledger, Partner Ledger, Budget vs. Actual, and Depreciation Schedules.
• Bank reconciliation with transaction matching, multi-currency support, and fiscal year/period management.
• Accounts Payable & Receivable: vendor bills, bill payments, invoice aging, and aged payables tracking.
• Fixed asset management with configurable depreciation methods, maintenance tracking, and disposal workflows.
• Budgeting engine with monthly allocations, variance analysis, and KPI dashboards.
• Analytic accounting with cost centers, distribution rules, and cross-dimensional reporting.

Sales & CRM
────────────
• End-to-end sales pipeline: leads, opportunities, quotations, sales orders, and invoicing.
• Customer management with contact segmentation, communication history, and activity tracking.
• Configurable approval workflows for quotes and orders with multi-step authorization chains.

Inventory & Supply Chain
────────────────────────
• Multi-warehouse inventory with real-time stock levels, lot/serial tracking, and automated reorder points.
• Purchase management: purchase orders, goods receipts, vendor management, and backorder handling.
• Stock valuation with FIFO/LIFO/average costing methods.

Point of Sale (POS)
───────────────────
• Full-featured POS terminal with barcode scanning, split payments, discounts, and receipt printing.
• Restaurant mode: interactive floor plan with table management, kitchen display system (KDS) with real-time order routing by station (kitchen, bar, grill, dessert), table session tracking, and guest management.
• Booking/reservation system with calendar integration.
• Shift management with cash-up reconciliation.

Business Intelligence & Spreadsheets
─────────────────────────────────────
• Built-in spreadsheet engine with formula support (powered by HyperFormula), cell formatting, embedded charts, and real-time multi-user collaboration.
• Shareable spreadsheets with granular permission controls (view/edit/admin), token-based access links, expiration policies, and public access toggles.
• Scheduled report automation: generates and emails financial/operational reports (PDF, CSV, Excel) on configurable schedules — daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
• Interactive dashboards with drill-down analytics and customizable KPI cards.

AI-Powered Features
───────────────────
• Multi-provider AI integration (configurable API keys with automatic failover and rate-limit handling).
• AI-assisted bank transaction categorization with confidence scoring.
• Intelligent financial insights caching for performance optimization.

Platform & Operations
─────────────────────
• Comprehensive audit trail with before/after snapshots, IP logging, and user attribution.
• Automated server actions with configurable triggers, multi-step workflows, retry logic, and execution logging.
• Document management with file attachments, storage buckets, and access policies.
• Admin tooling: platform-wide user management, email broadcasting, and system configuration.

Technology Stack
────────────────
React 18 · TypeScript · Vite · Tailwind CSS · shadcn/ui · Framer Motion · TanStack Query · Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Edge Functions, Realtime, Storage) · HyperFormula · Recharts · PDF generation · PWA-ready

This system is fully deployed in production, not a demo or prototype. It is architected for real-world multi-business use with enterprise-grade data integrity, security, and scalability.
