Smart Grow Empowerment is a small traditional microfinance institution operating on the ASA model (like ASA International Kenya). This governs all microfinance domain design:

- **Individual lending in a group setting**: each loan has exactly one client obligor; NO joint-liability group loans. Group membership is required to borrow but creates no shared liability.
- **Group meetings are the collection point**: fixed day/time/place per group; the loan officer collects repayments at the meeting in person. Repayment entry is a staff-side batch-per-meeting workflow with per-member receipts.
- **Staff-only system**: no client portal, no client login, no online self-application, no self-service repayment. Applications are created by staff for KYC'd, identified clients.
- **Physical screening**: assessment is a recorded in-person business visit by officer + branch manager; approval is branch-level, attributable, within authority limits. No algorithmic scoring or auto-approval.
- **Cycle graduation**: first loans are small and policy-capped; limits rise only after a successfully completed cycle, derived from the client's repayment history.
- **Legacy data**: inherited AccrualFlow ERP rows (invoices, bills, POS, payroll, CRM, inventory) are NOT this institution's data — never build microfinance surfaces over them. The 14 payroll `%loan%` tables are employee-advance artifacts scheduled for deletion, not lending tables.
