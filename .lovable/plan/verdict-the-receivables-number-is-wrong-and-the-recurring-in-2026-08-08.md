# Verdict: the receivables number is wrong, and the recurring invoice is unpayable — two distinct root causes

Both were confirmed against the live database, not inferred.

## What actually happened in your data

Customer Fredrick Mureti, one company, one branch:

```text
INV-00001   9,000   paid by receipt RCP-000001 (9,000)          -> settled
00002      20,000   receipt RCP-000002 (5,000)
                    + credit note CN-00001 applied (9,000)
                    + credit note CN-00002 applied (6,000)      -> fully settled
00003       1,800   paid by receipt RCP-000003 (1,800)          -> settled
00004         150   generated by "Generate now" from the
                    recurring template, posted to GL            -> genuinely open
```

The general ledger is **correct**: the AR control account nets to exactly 150.00
(30,950 invoiced, 15,800 received in cash, 15,000 settled by credit-note
application). The invoice records are also correct: 00002 shows amount_paid
20,000 and status paid.

So the 150 is real and expected. The 15,000 is **not** a real receivable —
it is a reporting defect.

## Root cause 1 — the AR/AP open-items projection only recognises cash settlement

`finance_ar_open_items` computes each invoice's residual as
`total - SUM(payment_allocations.amount)`. It does not subtract
`credit_note_applications`. Invoice 00002 therefore appears in the projection
with residual 15,000 **even though its own status in the same query result is
"paid"** — a self-contradicting row. Everything downstream inherits it:
`get_ar_summary` (the Finance → Receivables total), `get_ar_ap_aging_from_ledger`
(the aging report and the AR page list), customer statements and dashboards all
read these two views.

This contradicts the module's own stated contract: `src/services/finance/openItems.ts`
documents these views as "GL-gated projections" and the single source of AR/AP
truth. They are gated on the invoice *having* a journal entry, but the settled
amount is taken from one settlement channel only, so the projection has silently
diverged from the subledger it claims to summarise.

`finance_ap_open_items` has the identical defect on the payables side: residual is
`total - SUM(bill_payment_allocations.amount)`, ignoring
`vendor_credit_note_applications`. Same class of bug, same blast radius, so it is
fixed in the same pass.

Accounting principle being violated: a receivable is extinguished by *any*
qualifying settlement event — cash, customer credit, write-off, refund offset —
not only by cash. Mature systems (Odoo's `amount_residual` from reconciled
account-move-lines, NetSuite's applied-transaction model, SAP's cleared items)
derive residual from the reconciliation of ledger lines, which is why credit memos
close invoices there automatically.

## Root cause 2 — two invoice status vocabularies, and payability decided by status string

The recurring engine (`generate_recurring_invoice_occurrence`) posts its invoice
with `status = 'confirmed'`. The rest of the app treats `'sent'` as *the*
post-confirmation status: `useInvoicesPaginated.confirmInvoice` explicitly
overwrites `'confirmed'` with `'sent'` after the RPC, with a comment stating
"confirmed is treated as sent" — so manually confirmed invoices land on `sent`
and recurring invoices stay on `confirmed`.

Every payment surface then gates on a hard-coded status list, and none of the
three lists include `confirmed`:

- `src/components/sales/RecordPaymentDialog.tsx:157` — `.in("status", ["sent","partial","overdue"])`
- `src/components/invoices/RecordPaymentDialog.tsx` — driven by the caller
- `src/components/invoices/InvoiceListTable.tsx:174` — `["sent","viewed","partial","overdue"].includes(...)`

There is no shared constant; the lists disagree with each other on `viewed`, and
`ManagementReports.tsx:35` keeps a fourth variant. Meanwhile the settlement engine
`record_multi_invoice_payment` would happily accept invoice 00004 — the database
never refused it, the UI simply never offered it.

Deeper cause: payability is being inferred from a *workflow label* instead of from
the *residual balance of a posted document*. Any status the label-list authors did
not anticipate makes a real receivable unpayable and invisible.

## Not a root cause (explicitly cleared)

- The recurring engine is not writing a duplicate/parallel invoice or GL path:
  invoice 00004 has `source = 'recurring'`, `source_recurring_id`, billing period
  stamped, and a normal `source_type = 'invoice'` journal entry through the
  canonical posting engine.
- `amount_paid` was not corrupted. The only writers are the canonical settlement
  RPC, the void path (recompute from live allocations) and the credit-application
  RPC. No path sets it to the invoice total.
- The credit-note application RPC posts correctly: Dr Customer Credits / Cr AR
  (JE-00011, JE-00012). The ledger is right; only the projection ignores it.

# Plan

Ordered by financial risk. No UI redesign; no new engines.

## Phase 1 — Make the open-items projection recognise every settlement channel

Single migration, both sides:

- Rewrite `finance_ar_open_items` so `applied_amount` = non-voided
  `payment_allocations` + `credit_note_applications` (guarded against
  cancelled/voided credit notes), and exclude documents whose status is already
  terminal-settled, so a `paid` invoice can never surface as open.
- Rewrite `finance_ap_open_items` symmetrically with
  `vendor_credit_note_applications`.
- Add a `finance_open_items_tieout` view that compares, per business, the sum of
  projection residuals against the AR/AP subledger net, so this class of drift
  becomes visible instead of silent. Wire it into the existing finance integrity
  surface rather than a new one.

`get_ar_summary`, `get_ap_summary` and `get_ar_ap_aging_from_ledger` need no
change — they read the views, so the Receivables total, aging, AR page list and
statements all correct themselves to 150.00 for this workspace.

## Phase 2 — One invoice status vocabulary, one canonical writer

- Settle on `'sent'` as the single post-confirmation open status (the app already
  normalises to it, and it matches Odoo "open" / Xero "awaiting payment").
- Change `generate_recurring_invoice_occurrence` to land its auto-confirmed
  invoice on the same status as a manual confirmation instead of `'confirmed'`.
- Add a database-side backfill for existing `confirmed` invoices with a posted
  journal entry and residual > 0, so already-generated recurring invoices such as
  00004 become payable without manual intervention.
- Remove the client-side `confirmed -> sent` overwrite in
  `useInvoicesPaginated.confirmInvoice` once the RPC returns the canonical status,
  so there is one writer of the post-confirm status rather than two.

## Phase 3 — Payability derived from balance, not from a status label

- Introduce one shared module exporting the open/payable predicate and status set,
  derived as: posted journal entry exists AND residual > 0 AND status not in
  (draft, cancelled, voided).
- Replace the three divergent inline status arrays (`sales/RecordPaymentDialog`,
  `invoices/RecordPaymentDialog` caller, `InvoiceListTable` row action) with that
  predicate, and add the missing server-side balance filter to the customer
  invoice picker so zero-balance rows can never be offered.
- Source the Receive Payment picker from the corrected open-items projection
  rather than a status-filtered `invoices` query, so the dialog and the
  Receivables page can never disagree again.

## Phase 4 — Guardrails so this cannot regress

- SQL test asserting: invoice fully settled by credit notes disappears from
  `finance_ar_open_items`; bill fully settled by a vendor credit disappears from
  `finance_ap_open_items`; projection residual total equals subledger net.
- SQL test asserting a recurring-generated auto-confirmed invoice is payable by
  `record_multi_invoice_payment` and appears in the AR projection.
- Architecture ratchet test banning new hard-coded invoice-status arrays used as
  payability gates outside the shared module.

## Technical notes

- Views are recreated, not dropped-and-renamed, to preserve dependent
  functions (`get_ar_summary`, `get_ap_summary`,
  `get_ar_ap_aging_from_ledger`) and their grants.
- No data repair is required for the reported symptom: the invoice rows and the
  ledger are already correct, so Phase 1 alone restores the 150.00 figure. The
  Phase 2 backfill only touches the workflow status of already-posted recurring
  invoices.
- Nothing in the recurring subsystem gains its own posting, numbering, document or
  notification path; the only change there is the status value it writes.
